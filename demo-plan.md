# Planned Final Demo

## Demo Objective

Demonstrate that Sahaayak can process an emergency interaction locally on the smartphone without relying on an active internet connection.

---

# Demo Scenario

A person is trekking in a remote area.

Their friend falls and becomes unresponsive.

There is no mobile network available.

---

# Demo Flow

## Step 1 — Establish Offline Condition

Show the iQOO device.

Enable Airplane Mode.

The purpose is to demonstrate that the core workflow does not depend on internet connectivity.

---

## Step 2 — Open Sahaayak

Launch the application.

The home screen displays:

- Offline status
- Emergency start button
- Voice interaction option

---

## Step 3 — Describe the Emergency

The user speaks:

> "My friend fell while trekking and he is unconscious."

The application receives the voice input.

---

## Step 4 — Local Processing

The application processes the speech locally.

The local AI layer extracts emergency information.

Example:

```text
Incident: Fall
Conscious: No
Breathing: Unknown
```

---

## Step 5 — Follow-Up Question

The application identifies missing information.

Example:

> "Is the person breathing?"

The user responds.

---

## Step 6 — Protocol Selection

The structured emergency state is passed to the protocol engine.

The application enters the relevant emergency guidance flow.

---

## Step 7 — Guidance

The application provides:

- Large visual instructions
- Spoken guidance
- One step at a time

---

## Step 8 — Emergency Timeline

The application displays the important events recorded during the session.

Example:

```text
Emergency Started
Fall Reported
Person Unresponsive
Breathing Confirmed
Guidance Started
```

---

# Final Message

The demo concludes by showing:

```text
📵 No Internet Required
🎤 Voice First
🧠 Local AI
🚨 Structured Emergency Guidance
📱 Built for iQOO
```

---

# Demo Success Criteria

The final demonstration should clearly prove:

1. The application runs on the iQOO device.
2. The core workflow works in Airplane Mode.
3. Voice input is accepted.
4. Emergency context is processed locally.
5. The protocol engine controls the flow.
6. Guidance is delivered through the phone.ss
